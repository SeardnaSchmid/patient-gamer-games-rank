    How the scores work

    Patient Gamer (0–100): 25 for 2+ years since release, 25 for a current price of $10 or less, 15 if today's price is at the all-time low, 25 for quality (Metascore and Steam % positive averaged), 5 if Steam players rate it higher than critics, 5 for a complete/definitive edition, −15 for live service.

    Gamer Dad (0–100): 30 for a main story of 10 hours or less, 25 for quality, 15 for Steam Deck Verified (8 for Playable), 10 for full controller support (5 partial), 10 for Steam Cloud saves, 10 for the Steam "Save Anytime" tag, −20 for live service.
