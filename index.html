    How the scores work

    Patient games are out for at least a year and finished: not Early Access, not live service. The page shows only those. The Early Access filter can also show games that have been in Early Access for a year or more; they carry an "Early Access" badge.

    Worth the wait (0–100), how good is it today? Read it like a review score: 60 % players, 40 % critics. Players is the average of every player rating with at least 50 votes, each weighted by the number of digits of its vote count (1,000 votes weigh 3, a million weigh 6, so more votes count more without one site drowning out the others): Steam reviews of the last 6 months (all of them if there are too few, adjusted for how many there are), Metacritic user score, GOG and HowLongToBeat ratings, each scaled to 0–100. Critics is the Metascore. A missing source is left out; if a side is missing entirely, the other counts alone. Being finished is what makes a game patient, and the change since launch is on the card; neither adds points.

    Gamer Dad (0–100), does it fit a busy life? 30 for a short main story (full at 10 hours or less, nothing at 60 or more; HowLongToBeat), 25 for quality (full at Worth the wait 95, nothing at 70 or less), 15 for Steam Deck Verified (8 for Playable), 10 for full controller support (5 partial), 10 for Steam Cloud saves, 10 for the Steam "Save Anytime" tag.

    Multiplayer (0–100, shown on cards when the Multiplayer filter is on; "Active online" means 50 or more), is the community still there? 35 for players (log scale, 10k peak a day is full), 25 for review activity in the last 6 months compared with the 6 before, 25 for quality now, 15 for an announcement from the developers within the last 3 months.
