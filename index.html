    How the scores work

    Patient games are out for at least a year and finished: not Early Access, not live service. The page shows only those unless you pick "All games".

    Worth the wait (0–100), is it good and finished today? 50 for quality now (Steam reviews of the last 6 months, adjusted for how many there are), 25 for how reviews moved since the launch quarter (held up 15, fixed since launch 25), 15 for being out a year and not Early Access or live service, 10 for the Metascore.

    Buy now (0–100), is today the moment? 40 for the discount off Steam's list price (75 % off or more), 40 for being at the all-time low (CheapShark), 20 for being below the price the game usually sits at. That last part needs 30 days of price history tracked here; until then the other two count 50 each. Free games have no deal score.

    Two other lenses, not the focus: Gamer Dad (0–100), does it fit a busy schedule? 30 for a main story of 10 hours or less, 25 for quality now, 15 for Steam Deck Verified (8 for Playable), 10 for full controller support (5 partial), 10 for Steam Cloud saves, 10 for the Steam "Save Anytime" tag, −20 for live service.

    Multiplayer (0–100, games with online play only), is the community still there? 35 for players (log scale, 10k peak a day is full), 25 for review activity in the last 6 months compared with the 6 before, 25 for quality now, 15 for an announcement from the developers within the last 3 months.
