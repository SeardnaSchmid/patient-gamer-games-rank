    How the scores work

    Patient Gamer (0–100): 35 for being 2+ years old, 30 for a sale price of $10 or less, 20 for quality (average of critic and user score), 5 if users rate it higher than critics did at launch, 10 for a complete edition, −15 for live service.

    Gamer Dad (0–100): 35 for a main story of 10 hours or less, 30 for quality, 20 if it works in short sessions, 15 for handheld (Switch / Steam Deck), −20 for live service.

    The scores, prices and playtimes are rough figures entered by hand. Check current prices before you buy.
